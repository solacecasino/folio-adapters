For [Folio 1.0.1](https://github.com/solacecasino/folio/releases/tag/v1.0.1).

- **Folio Link 0.3:** invites are now links (`folio://join#…`). Click one and Folio opens and joins. Older `folio-link:` invites still work. Copying and pasting invites now works on **Windows and Linux** too (it only worked on macOS before).
- **PDF Export 0.2.1, Writing Tools 0.2.1:** each links to its homepage from Folio's Adapters window.
- The catalog lists **WebAssembly plugins** too, and the "List my adapter or plugin" form takes both.

Install: download the zip for your computer, then drop it on Folio's window.
